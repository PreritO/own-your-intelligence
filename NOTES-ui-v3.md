# NOTES ui-v3

Calls I made without a human:
- **Collapsed quest box.** By default the top-centre panel holds only the input, **▶ Commission** and a "▾ Example quests" toggle. The drawer holds the 6 cards plus the old sub-line and the Demo/Replay buttons. It folds on any commission (card or typed), on a `spawn` (a quest starts), and when the Gym button is pressed. Only the viewer's own toggles are saved (`localStorage["mp:quest-examples-open"]`, try/catch); automatic folds are not a preference.
- **Overview framing.** `controls.ts` (not mine) keeps a reference to the exported `HUD_INSETS` object and reads `.top` on every overview pose. The UI measures the *collapsed* bar on its first frame, sets `HUD_INSETS.top = bottom + 24` (about 99 px instead of 380), and fires `CAMERA_EVENTS.home`. Expanding never moves the camera. The north wings are back in frame. Integrator: consider a real `setInsets` hook in `api.ts`.
- **Gym button** sits in the party bar next to ⌂ Map. It dispatches `mp:gym-go`. `rooms/gym.ts` flies there with `flyTo` and opens the board. Map, follow, a commission or Esc end the fly.
- **Leaderboard = DOM overlay** (`web/src/ui/leaderboard.ts`), shown whenever the camera is within 30 m of the Gym (hidden beyond 38 m), with or without `?demo`. It re-reads `gym-scoreboard.json` every 15 s while open.
- **Per-agent cards** (integrator's direction): one card per team in `gym-scoreboard.json`, with a base → gen1 → gen2 → gen3 ladder. Grounded % is the bar, followed by correct % and latency with ▲/▼ versus the previous generation. Claude is a dashed line plus a reference chip. The job id shows on hover. A `status: "training"` row renders as "genN training on River…". The champion is the `promoted` row, else the best by grounded; it is labelled "best", not "promoted". The party bar shows "River genN" under an agent only when a row is `promoted: true` (the palace nameplates are presence-owned).
- **Data:** `fixtures/river-runs.json` is copied from the ws/training logs (`server/train/out/*.log`). The run-2 held-out size of 16 comes from rl.py's `--eval-every` help text. The `--simple` runs have no end line, so they are marked `partial`. `fixtures/gym-scoreboard.json` holds river-serve's real Legal rows as given by the integrator. river-serve overwrites it later.
- **3D scoreboard** without `?demo` now draws the real `gym-legal.jsonl` curve at once. With `?demo` it still animates. Local-sim checkpoints (`sim`, `sim-*`) are labelled "local RL sim … not River". Previously `"sim"` alone slipped through as "River RL".
- `quest-onboarding.prev.jsonl` is not scored: it is an older copy of the same quest.
