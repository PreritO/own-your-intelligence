P1 issues get a 30-minute first response and 4-hour target resolution, with uptime credits for missed 99.5% monthly commitment. Owner: Keiko Ferrante (Head of Customer Success).

| Priority | Definition | First response | Target resolution |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| P1 | ordering or counts unavailable for a location | 30 minutes, 24/7 | 4 hours |
| P2 | a feature broken, workaround exists | 4 business hours | 2 business days |
| P3 | question or minor bug | 1 business day | best effort |

Enterprise customers get a named CSM and a quarterly business review. Uptime commitment is 99.5% monthly; if we miss it the customer gets a credit of 5% of that month's fee for each 0.5% below target, capped at 25%.

Onboarding SLA: new Growth customers are live (menu loaded, first count done) within 14 days of signature.

Last reviewed July 7, 2026.
