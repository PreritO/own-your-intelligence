Refunds and credits outside the SLA formula are handled case by case. Customer Success requests the refund in the #billing channel with the account name, amount and reason; someone in Finance processes it in Ledgerline. Refunds over $1,000 need approval.

There is no written owner for this process and nobody has decided who approves refunds over $1,000 since the process was drafted. Last edited March 2026.
