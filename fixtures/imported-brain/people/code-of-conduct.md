Harassment of any kind is grounds for termination, and concerns should be reported to Ines Takahara or Noor Haddad. Owner: Ines Takahara.

We expect everyone to treat colleagues, customers and restaurant staff with respect. Harassment of any kind is grounds for termination. Report concerns to Ines Takahara (Head of People) or, if the concern involves Ines, to Noor Haddad directly. Reports can also be made anonymously through the Ethicspoint form linked in the People Notion space.
