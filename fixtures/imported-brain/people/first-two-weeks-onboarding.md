Benefits enrollment must be completed within 30 days of your start date, with Kitewing paying 90% of employee premiums. Owner: Ines Takahara.

1. Day 1: laptop pickup, Okta and Google accounts, security training (required before any customer data access, see the Information Security Policy).
2. Day 2 to 5: shadow a customer success call and a sales demo. Read the Sales Playbook even if you are an engineer.
3. Week 2: your manager sets your 30/60/90 plan in Lattice.

Benefits enrollment must be completed within 30 days of your start date. Health insurance is through Cobaltcare; Kitewing pays 90% of employee premiums and 60% for dependents.

See PTO and Leave for time off information.
