Kitewing has flexible PTO with a minimum of 15 days off per calendar year. Owner: Ines Takahara.

Kitewing has **flexible PTO** with a minimum: everyone takes at least 15 days off per calendar year. Managers get a nudge in Lattice in September if someone on their team has taken fewer than 8.

| Leave type | Allowance | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Flexible PTO | minimum 15 days/year | book in Lattice 2 weeks ahead for 3+ days |
| Company holidays | 11 days | list in the People calendar |
| Sick leave | unlimited | no doctor's note under 5 consecutive days |
| Parental leave | 16 weeks fully paid | for all parents, after 90 days of employment |
| Bereavement | up to 10 days | |

Winter shutdown: the office closes December 24, 2026 through January 1, 2027. On-call engineers who cover the shutdown get the same number of days back in January.

Questions go to Ines Takahara in #people-ops.
