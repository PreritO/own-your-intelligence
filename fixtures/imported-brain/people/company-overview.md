Kitewing builds Kitewing Pantry and serves 612 restaurant groups and 1,948 locations as of August 2026. Owner: Ines Takahara.

Kitewing, Inc. builds **Kitewing Pantry**, inventory and ordering software for independent restaurants and small restaurant groups. We were founded in 2023 by Noor Haddad (CEO) and Felix Arrowood (CTO). We raised a $9.5M Series A led by Northbay Ventures in February 2026. We are 31 people across Portland, Austin and remote.

Our customers are restaurant operators with 1 to 40 locations. As of August 2026 we serve 612 restaurant groups and 1,948 locations.

## How we work

- Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 Pacific. Outside those hours nobody expects a reply.
- Decisions are written down. If a decision was made in Slack, the person who made it posts it in #decisions within one business day.
- Every policy has an owner. If you can't find the owner of a policy, ask in #people-ops.
- We ship on Tuesdays and Thursdays. No deploys after 14:00 Pacific on Fridays.
