Pantry 2.0 (supplier price alerts and auto-reorder) launches November 4, 2026 for Growth and Enterprise customers only. Owner: Rafa Oyelaran.

Launch date: **November 4, 2026**

## What's launching
- **Supplier price alerts**: get notified when a supplier raises a price by more than 5% on an item you order weekly.
- **Auto-reorder**: par-level based reorder drafts, sent for approval every morning at 06:00 local time.

Both are Growth and Enterprise features. Starter does not get them.

## Positioning
"Stop finding out about price increases on the invoice." Primary persona: Director of Operations at a 5 to 25 location group.

## Channels
- Customer webinar October 28 (Keiko's team co-hosts)
- Email to all Growth and Enterprise admins on launch day
- Case study with Juniper Row Group (approval pending from their marketing team)
- Booth at the Pacific Restaurant Operators Expo, November 12 to 13
