Kitewing's 2026 pricing has Starter at $149/location/month, Growth at $299/location/month, and Enterprise custom with a $249/location floor.

Issued by Finance, January 20, 2026. Prices in USD. Billed annually unless noted.

| PLAN | PRICE | WHO IT'S FOR |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | $149 / location / month | 1 location, self-serve |
| Growth | $299 / location / month | 2 to 40 locations, AE-led |
| Enterprise | custom, floor $249/location | 40+ locations or custom integrations |

Starter can be billed monthly.

### One-time Fees
- Onboarding (Growth): $1,500 per location
- Onboarding (Enterprise): waived
- POS integration (non-standard POS): $2,000 per integration

### Included
- All plans: inventory counts, recipe costing, supplier catalog, mobile app.
- Growth adds: multi-location transfers, par levels, invoice scanning (500 invoices / location / month).
- Enterprise adds: SSO, custom roles, API access, dedicated CSM.

### Payment Terms
- Net 30. Annual prepay gets 10% off.
- Multi-year contracts need Finance approval.
