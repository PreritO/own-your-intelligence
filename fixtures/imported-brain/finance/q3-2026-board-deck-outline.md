ARR reached $4.1M at end of August with 17 months runway, and security/compliance has had no owner since July.

**Meeting date:** September 18, 2026. Prepared by Noor Haddad and Tobias Mbeki.

**Board:** Noor Haddad, Felix Arrowood, Carmen Delacroix (Northbay Ventures), independent seat vacant.

### Slide 1. Headline numbers
- ARR $4.1M at end of August, up from $3.2M at end of Q1.
- Net revenue retention 118%.
- Gross margin 74%.
- Cash $7.3M; monthly net burn $410k; runway 17 months.

### Slide 2. Pricing change
Growth tier moves from $299 to $349 per location per month for new customers from October 1, 2026 (decided in #pricing-decisions on September 9). Existing customers grandfathered through March 31, 2027.

### Slide 3. Pantry 2.0 launch
Supplier price alerts and auto-reorder ship November 4, 2026. Launch plan owned by Rafa Oyelaran.

### Slide 4. Risks
- Security and compliance: no owner since July. Three enterprise deals (Saltgrass Hospitality, Juniper Row Group, Marrow and Vine) are waiting on security questionnaires.
- The August 14 ordering outage cost 6 hours of order submission; postmortem done.

### Slide 5. Asks and commitments
- Board asked for a named security owner by the Q4 meeting.
- Tobias to present a 2027 operating plan at the December 10, 2026 board meeting.
- Approve hiring plan: 6 roles in Q4 (3 engineering, 2 sales, 1 security lead).
