Notes from the May 2026 review with outside counsel (Harlow & Pike LLP). Kept by Tobias Mbeki until we hire in-house counsel.

**Our paper:** Kitewing Customer MSA v4 plus an order form per deal. We sign customer paper only for groups over 40 locations.

Standard positions:
- Term: 12 months, auto-renews for 12 months unless cancelled 60 days before renewal.
- Liability cap: 12 months of fees paid. Uncapped only for breach of confidentiality.
- Payment: Net 30 (matches the pricing sheet).
- Data: customer owns its data; we delete within 60 days of termination (matches the security policy).
- SLA: 99.5% monthly uptime, service credits per the Support SLA.
- Governing law: Oregon.

Redlines that need Noor's sign-off: liability above 12 months of fees, MFN pricing clauses, any termination for convenience.

DPA: we have a DPA template for customers who ask. There's no signed DPA with our billing vendor Ledgerline yet, which counsel flagged as a gap.

Signature authority: Noor Haddad signs all customer contracts over $50k ARR. Wren Castellano signs under $50k ARR.
