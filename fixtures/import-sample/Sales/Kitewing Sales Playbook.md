**Kitewing Sales Playbook**

**Owner: Wren Castellano, VP Sales**
**Last updated: September 15, 2026**

# **1\. Who we sell to** {#1.-who-we-sell-to}

Ideal customer: independent restaurant groups with 3 to 40 locations, doing at least $2M annual revenue per location, currently tracking inventory in spreadsheets or a legacy POS add\-on. Single\-location restaurants are served self\-serve on the Starter plan and are not worked by AEs.

Buyer: owner/operator or Director of Operations. Champion: usually the executive chef or kitchen manager.

# **2\. Sales stages** {#2.-sales-stages}

| Stage | Exit criteria | Owner |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Discovery | pain confirmed, locations counted | SDR |
| Demo | live demo with their menu loaded | AE |
| Pilot | 30\-day pilot at 1 to 2 locations, success metric written down | AE \+ CS |
| Proposal | pricing approved by deal desk if discount \> 15% | AE |
| Closed won | order form signed, handed to CS within 2 business days | AE |

# **3\. Discounting rules** {#3.-discounting-rules}

* AEs may discount up to 15% without approval.
* 15% to 25% needs approval from Wren Castellano.
* Anything above 25%, or any contract longer than 24 months, needs Noor Haddad and Tobias Mbeki (finance).
* Never discount the onboarding fee. It is $1,500 per location for Growth and waived for Enterprise.

# **4\. Pricing** {#4.-pricing}

List prices are in the Pricing 2026 sheet (PDF from Finance). Note: pricing changes decided in #pricing\-decisions override the sheet until the sheet is reissued.

# **5\. Security reviews** {#5.-security-reviews}

About a third of groups over 10 locations send a security questionnaire. Send them the Information Security Policy and the SOC 2 status. We do not have a SOC 2 report yet; the Type I audit is planned for Q1 2027. Do not promise a date.

# **6\. Handoff to Customer Success** {#6.-handoff-to-customer-success}

Closed-won deals are handed to Keiko Ferrante's team in HubSpot with the pilot success metric copied into the onboarding ticket. Onboarding SLA is in the Support SLA doc.
