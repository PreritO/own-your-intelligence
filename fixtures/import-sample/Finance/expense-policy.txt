Kitewing Expense Policy
Owner: Tobias Mbeki (Head of Finance)
Effective: April 1, 2026

1. Submit expenses in Ledgerline within 30 days with a receipt for anything over $25.
2. Meals while travelling: up to $75 per day. Customer dinners: up to $120 per person, name the customer.
3. Flights: economy, booked at least 14 days ahead where possible. Premium economy allowed for flights over 6 hours.
4. Software purchases over $500 per year need approval from Finance and a security review if the tool touches customer data (see the Information Security Policy).
5. Company cards: issued to managers and AEs. Card limit $5,000 per month.
6. Approvals: your manager approves up to $2,000. Above $2,000, Tobias Mbeki approves. Above $10,000, Noor Haddad also approves.
7. Home-office stipend and internet reimbursement are described in the handbook.

Reimbursements are paid in the next payroll run after approval.
