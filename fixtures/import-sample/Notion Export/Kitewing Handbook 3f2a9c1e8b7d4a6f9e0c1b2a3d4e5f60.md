## Who we are

Kitewing, Inc. builds **Kitewing Pantry**, inventory and ordering software for independent restaurants and small restaurant groups. We were founded in 2023 by Noor Haddad (CEO) and Felix Arrowood (CTO). We raised a $9.5M Series A led by Northbay Ventures in February 2026. We are 31 people across Portland, Austin and remote.

Our customers are restaurant operators with 1 to 40 locations. As of August 2026 we serve 612 restaurant groups and 1,948 locations.

## How we work

- Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 Pacific. Outside those hours nobody expects a reply.
- Decisions are written down. If a decision was made in Slack, the person who made it posts it in #decisions within one business day.
- Every policy has an owner. If you can't find the owner of a policy, ask in #people-ops.
- We ship on Tuesdays and Thursdays. No deploys after 14:00 Pacific on Fridays.

## Remote work

Anyone can work remotely. Portland and Austin offices are open Monday to Thursday. The home-office stipend is $600 once, plus $50 per month for internet, paid through Ledgerline expenses (see the Expense Policy from Finance).

Team offsites happen twice a year. The next one is in Austin, October 20 to 22, 2026.

## Code of conduct

We expect everyone to treat colleagues, customers and restaurant staff with respect. Harassment of any kind is grounds for termination. Report concerns to Ines Takahara (Head of People) or, if the concern involves Ines, to Noor Haddad directly. Reports can also be made anonymously through the Ethicspoint form linked in the People Notion space.

## Your first two weeks

1. Day 1: laptop pickup, Okta and Google accounts, security training (required before any customer data access, see the Information Security Policy).
2. Day 2 to 5: shadow a customer success call and a sales demo. Read the Sales Playbook even if you are an engineer.
3. Week 2: your manager sets your 30/60/90 plan in Lattice.

Benefits enrollment must be completed within 30 days of your start date. Health insurance is through Cobaltcare; Kitewing pays 90% of employee premiums and 60% for dependents.
