Supplier of soft robotic grippers. Proposed a 12-month supply agreement at $40k for the Gripper v2 fingertip pads and actuators.

Gripworks is a 30-person gripper maker. Eng picked them after the [[eng/gripper-v2|Gripper v2]] bake-off for pad durability. The commercial terms are in the [[legal/gripworks-msa|Gripworks MSA]]; the spend will land on the Q4 supplier line. Vendor risk review passed in August (see [[eng/vendor-risk-reviews|Vendor Risk Reviews]]).
