Renewal of Kestrel Foods' 42-arm software and support contract, $1.4M ARR, due Dec 31. Kestrel asked for a 15% discount and a Gripper v2 upgrade path; they cite the Fresno controller failures.

Asks from Kestrel's VP Operations (Sep 17 call):

1. 15% off software and support for a 2-year renewal. Needs VP Sales approval under [[sales/deal-desk-rules|Deal Desk Rules]].
2. Upgrade credit toward [[eng/gripper-v2|Gripper v2]] for all 42 arms. No v2 price exists on the [[sales/pricing-sheet|Pricing Sheet]].
3. Service credits for the Fresno downtime under the [[support/sla-policy|SLA Policy]]; the incident is [[support/arm-controller-escalation|Arm Controller Escalation]].
4. A named security contact for their annual vendor review.

Renewal risk is rated high on the [[support/customer-health-scorecard|Customer Health Scorecard]]. Losing Kestrel would cut runway by about 2 months in the [[finance/runway-forecast|Runway Forecast]] base case.
