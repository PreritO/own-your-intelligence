Discount and terms approvals: reps up to 10% off list, VP Sales up to 20%, CEO above 20%. Multi-year prepay, custom SLAs and non-standard legal terms need Finance and Legal review.

| Request | Approver |
| --- | --- |
| Discount up to 10% | Account executive |
| Discount 10-20% | VP Sales (Owen Achterberg) |
| Discount above 20% | CEO ([[people/mara-okafor|Mara Okafor]]) |
| Custom SLA above standard | Head of Customer Success |
| Non-standard legal terms | Legal, per [[legal/contract-playbook|Contract Playbook]] |
| Contracts over $30k | an authorized signatory, per [[people/signatories|Authorized Signatories]] |

Every discount above 10% must show the effect on hardware margin against the [[finance/gross-margin-plan|Gross Margin Plan]]. Custom uptime or response commitments are checked against the [[support/sla-policy|SLA Policy]].
