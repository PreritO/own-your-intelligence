Hardware warranty for the arm: 12 months parts and labor from install, taken from the 2024 order form. No Gripper v2 warranty is recorded. Owner: not recorded.

Support applies the 12-month arm warranty to controller boards, castings and battery packs. Nobody in Legal has written warranty terms for [[eng/gripper-v2|Gripper v2]]: pad wear life, actuator coverage and whether upgraded Gripper v1 customers inherit their remaining term are all open. Legal's [[legal/contract-playbook|Contract Playbook]] covers vendor contracts only. The supplier side is [[legal/gripworks-msa|Gripworks MSA]], which has no warranty pass-through clause.

| Gripper v2 warranty | none defined |
