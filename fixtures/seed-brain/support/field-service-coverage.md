Two field technicians cover the US West Coast directly; everywhere else uses spares shipped overnight plus remote guidance. EU sites get spares in 2 to 3 days.

| Region | Coverage | Onsite target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| US West | 2 Acme technicians (Fresno, Reno base) | 24-48 h |
| US other | spares + remote | 3 business days |
| EU | spares + remote | 3-5 business days |

Spare boards and grippers ship from stock in [[ops/inventory-2026-09|Inventory]] via [[companies/axis-logistics|Axis Logistics]]; EU spares follow [[legal/export-controls|Export Controls]]. Premium customers outside US West cannot get the 48-hour onsite in the [[support/sla-policy|SLA Policy]] today.
