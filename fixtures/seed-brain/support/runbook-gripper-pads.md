Replacing worn Gripper v1 fingertip pads: inspect weekly, replace at 1.5 mm tread depth or visible tearing, about 10 minutes per gripper. Pads are a consumable billed at $95 per set outside the first 90 days.

1. Check tread depth with the gauge in the site kit; replace below 1.5 mm.
2. Order sets from spares stock listed in [[ops/inventory-2026-09|Inventory]].
3. Tears inside 90 days go in the [[ops/quality-rma-log|Quality and RMA Log]] as defects, not wear.
4. [[eng/gripper-v2|Gripper v2]] pads from [[companies/gripworks|Gripworks]] use a different mount; this runbook does not cover them yet.
