Support escalation tiers, written in February: Tier 1 support engineers, Tier 2 field service, Tier 3 engineering on-call. Sev-1 goes straight to Tier 3; Sev-2 escalates after 4 hours without a workaround.

Tier 3 pages the eng on-call alias from the [[eng/oncall-rotation|On-call Rotation]] and opens an incident under the [[eng/incident-response|Incident Response Plan]]. Hardware trends (three or more arms at one site) also notify manufacturing. This page still lists the old support hotline and the Halden Compliance contact for security questions, both retired since it was written. Field visits are scheduled through [[support/field-service-coverage|Field Service Coverage]].
