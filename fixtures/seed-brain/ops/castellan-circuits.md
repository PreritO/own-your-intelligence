Contract manufacturer that assembles the arm controller board. No quality agreement is on record, and no supply agreement: Acme buys on individual purchase orders only.

Castellan builds boards in lots of 60; lot CB-2607 (July) is the lot behind most Q3 controller RMAs. Without a quality agreement there are no agreed defect rates, no root-cause turnaround and no cost recovery for field failures. POs follow Finance's [[finance/purchase-order-policy|Purchase Order Policy]]. Castellan never went through [[legal/vendor-onboarding|Vendor Onboarding]] because it predates the process.
