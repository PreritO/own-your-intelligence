Contracts over $30k need the CEO (Mara Okafor) or COO (Dev Lindqvist) to sign. Contracts up to $30k can be signed by the General Counsel (Tomas Reyes) once budget is confirmed.

Signature authority was set by the board in [[legal/board-consents|Board Consents]]. Before any signature, the contract must be approved in the [[legal/approvals|Legal Approvals Log]] and Finance must confirm the budget line. Purchase orders follow a separate threshold in the [[finance/purchase-order-policy|Purchase Order Policy]].

| Contract value | Who may sign |
| --- | --- |
| up to $30k | Tomas Reyes (GC) |
| over $30k | Mara Okafor (CEO) or Dev Lindqvist (COO) |
