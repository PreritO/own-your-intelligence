Legal's standard positions for vendor contracts: Net-45 payment, liability cap at 1x fees, 12-month default term, Delaware law.

Every vendor contract goes through [[legal/vendor-onboarding|Vendor Onboarding]] first, then is approved in the [[legal/approvals|Legal Approvals Log]]. NDAs use the [[legal/nda-template|NDA Template]] unchanged.
