Steps to onboard a new supplier: NDA, vendor risk review, contract per playbook, budget confirmation from Finance, then signature.

1. Mutual NDA ([[legal/nda-template|NDA Template]]).
2. Security questionnaire in [[eng/vendor-risk-reviews|Vendor Risk Reviews]].
3. Contract drafted per the [[legal/contract-playbook|Contract Playbook]].
4. Finance confirms the budget line and PO route ([[finance/purchase-order-policy|Purchase Order Policy]]).
5. Signature by an [[people/signatories|authorized signatory]].
