POs up to $25k are approved by the Controller; POs over $25k need the VP Finance (Raj Patel). A PO is not a contract signature.

Controller: [[people/iris-kowalczyk|Iris Kowalczyk]]. VP Finance: [[people/raj-patel|Raj Patel]]. Contract signatures follow [[people/signatories|Authorized Signatories]] instead.
